# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, specific, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and evidence,
not people. Respect privacy and do not post confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption,
and publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the security contact process in
`SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This compact policy is based on the Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles.
